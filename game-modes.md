Civ13's Nomads and TDM servers host modes and maps chosen through votes. Read the lobby and round briefing: the selected scenario sets roles, equipment, relationships and objectives.

## Build and roleplay

| Mode | What you do | Player guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nomads and civilizations | Gather, build, research and form a society with other players. | [Nomads](https://civ13.github.io/civ13-wiki/gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.html) |
| Colony Building RP | Establish a settlement through assigned trades, supplies and cooperation. | [Colony building](https://civ13.github.io/civ13-wiki/gamemodes/Colony_Building.html) |
| Prison Simulation | Play a prisoner or guard within the selected setting and its roleplay rules. | [Prison roleplay](https://civ13.github.io/civ13-wiki/gamemodes/Prison_Roleplay.html) |
| The Art of the Deal | Work for a company, negotiate and trade with rivals. | [The Art of the Deal](https://civ13.github.io/civ13-wiki/gamemodes/The_Art_of_the_Deal.html) |

## Events

We sometimes run events around these modes and other scenarios. Follow [Civ13 Discord announcements](https://discord.gg/hBEtg4x) for the server, map, time, participation instructions and rules. Check the announcement before joining; this page does not list a fixed schedule.

Events can use arena bouts, roleplay settings, crew scenarios or survival objectives. Special scenarios also include **event-city Hunt**, **Player vs NPCs / Cursed Island**, and **Lab breach**. Read the [Events Guide](https://civ13.github.io/civ13-wiki/gamemodes/Events.html) for their public briefings and preparation. Event-city Hunt differs from the shipwrecked-pirate Hunt map's Survival RP.

Use the [wiki's mode overview](https://civ13.github.io/civ13-wiki/gamemodes/Game_Modes.html) and [map catalog](https://civ13.github.io/civ13-wiki/maps_overview.html) to find scenario details. [Server rules](https://civ13.github.io/civ13-wiki/rules/Rules.html) apply during events too.
